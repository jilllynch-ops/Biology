How a turn works

  1. A scenario is drawn. That change is the stimulus.
  2. Pick one receptor, one control center and one or two responses from the card bank.
  3. Name the system and the feedback type.
  4. Check your loop. You earn up to 5 points: 1 each for the receptor, control center, response, system and feedback type.

Watch out for decoy cards. They are wrong on purpose.
